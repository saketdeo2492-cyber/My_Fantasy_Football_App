DATA: official FPL API, fetched server-side through this app's own proxy — no third-party relay involved. Prices in £m, Form = avg pts over last 30 days. Connecting exchanges an FPL OIDC refresh token you provide for a short-lived access token used to fetch your exact bank and free transfers — the token is kept in an httpOnly cookie your browser's JavaScript can't read, and is never logged. Without connecting, Free Transfers is an estimate reconstructed from public transfer history instead. Recommender projects a position-specific points score per player across the next 4 gameweeks (nearer weeks weighted more), using clean-sheet odds, expected goals/assists, defensive-contribution thresholds, form, and fixture difficulty — double/blank gameweeks are detected and weighted or flagged accordingly. Suggested swaps are checked for budget and the 3-per-club limit against the resulting squad; anything beyond your free transfers is only shown if the projected gain clearly clears the -4 hit. Players who recently switched clubs or are short on minutes there get a "New Signing / Limited Minutes" flag — season stats can be borrowed from a different club, and no stats-only model can predict squad role or manager trust, so this is a caution to research further, not a verdict; flagged players are shown with a down-weighted score if suggested as a transfer target, never ruled out outright.
