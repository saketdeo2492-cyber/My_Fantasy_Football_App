DATA: official FPL API, fetched server-side through this app's own proxy — no third-party relay involved. Prices in £m, Form = avg pts over last 30 days. Connecting exchanges an FPL OIDC refresh token you provide for a short-lived access token used to fetch your exact bank and free transfers — the token is kept in an httpOnly cookie your browser's JavaScript can't read, and is never logged.
